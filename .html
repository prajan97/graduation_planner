How this is worked out. Modules start on the first Monday of each month and run for eight weeks. Full-time students join a new module every month, so from week 5 of one module to week 8 you are carrying two at once. Part-time students join every other month, which removes the overlap entirely.

Module rotation and assessment weightings are taken from Walbrook's published schedule for the general online MBA. Final project dates are indicative and depend on the length your programme sets. Confirm anything you're relying on with the programme team before you commit to it.
